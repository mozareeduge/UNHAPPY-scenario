# Changelog

## v2.6.0 — brain/body integration: annexation, bridge, erosion, exhaustion, closure

Retired the persistent Project Relay v4.3 repository control plane in favor of externally
supplied, per-session execution packages; `CLAUDE.md` now holds only durable repository
boundaries. Implemented the approved atelier decisions (`R2` visual verdicts) on top of
v2.4.0 while preserving the locked system-message core, its wording/order/timing, and the
standalone, no-network, no-storage architecture.

Adopted:

- **Poem field annexation** — the poem field annexes space from the messenger/failure field
  on desktop as failures accumulate, driven by a cycle- and inscription-derived `--annex`
  level; shell actions, composer, and outgoing message remain unobstructed.
- **Failed-transmission bridge** — a restrained, event-bound, pointer-inert visual line
  connects each failure source (attachment error, message failure, or active shell) to its
  corresponding poem inscription at the moment the line is queued.
- **Historical shell pressure body** — retired shells carry measurable, age-derived tonal
  displacement (inset shadow, brightness) while remaining textless, inert, and unreadable.
- **Line-anchored bloom** — each blood/diffuse bloom is now anchored to the real rendered
  geometry of its own poem line (via `Range` client rects) instead of a pseudo-random offset.
- **Process travel exhaustion** — `Reconnecting…` and `Reporting the problem…` expend less
  kinetic amplitude and signal opacity across repeated cycles, derived from `cycleId`, with a
  static (non-animated) fallback under reduced motion; all durations are unchanged.
- **Decision-field parity** — `Yes` and `No` receive identical class, size, and visual
  treatment; DOM order and default focus on `No` are unchanged.
- **Mobile fault seam** — added tactile `:active` feedback to the existing fault-latch;
  44×44 target, tap/drag, and unclipped geometry at small viewports are preserved.
- **Retry credibility erosion** — every `Try again` becomes visibly, deterministically less
  confident across cycles (reduced halo, glow, and scale ceiling) while remaining fully
  operable and meeting practical hit-target size; no duration changes.
- **No-branch nonverbal closure** — choosing `No` now visibly closes the question shell
  (fade, dim, no new words) entirely inside the existing, unmodified 650 ms interval before
  the renewed `Connection attempt failed`.

Preserved: immediate poem emergence, diffuse bloom subordinate to text, the eight locked
system-message lines, all locked wait durations, and the standalone/no-network/no-storage
privacy model.

Declined (not implemented, per the creative implementation contract): ambient event-driven
optical field modulation, attachment retinal afterimage, and reporting-failure depth collapse.

Extended `tests/qa.py` with focused deterministic coverage for every objectively testable
C01–C09 predicate (24 v2.4 checks retained, 9 new checks added) and `tests/render_visuals.py`
with named review renders for retry/process cycling, the decision/closure moment, dense
historical pressure, desktop annexation, and line/bloom anchoring.

## v2.4.0 — spatial composition and mobile-reading correction

- Re-engineered the desktop composition at wide, compact-desktop, tablet, and short-height viewports.
- Reserved a dedicated failure stage above the surviving outgoing message, preventing shells from drifting into the composer or poem field.
- Added shell-specific minimum and maximum dimensions so questions and actions remain proportionate and never collapse vertically.
- Reduced the visible procedural archive to six historical frames plus one active frame.
- Reorganized historical frames around the active shell in one authored direction, producing a readable accumulation rather than scattered offsets.
- Preserved the black, bone, rust, blood-light, restrained linework, typography, and material atmosphere of v2.3.0.
- Added a visible mobile fault-latch integrated into the poem boundary. Its illuminated node and directional chevrons show that the underfield can be expanded and collapsed.
- Changed mobile tap behavior to toggle directly between compact and reading states; drag remains available for the expanded state.
- Fixed the mobile fault control being clipped by the poem field and verified its actual pointer target.
- Added dedicated QA for short desktop height, ordered residue geometry, and mobile underfield affordance.
